> **Supernatural Gift: Hellfire Channeling (1/Day)**  
> You can draw upon infernal hellfire to empower your fire-based spells.
> 
> - **Activation:** Choose a number between 1 and 5 as your Hellfire Level.
> - **Control Check:** Roll a Charisma saving throw with a DC of **10 + (Hellfire Level × 2)** to maintain control over the hellfire.
> 
> **Outcomes:**
> 
> - **Success:** Add **Hellfire Level d6** fire damage to the damage roll of your spell. If the spell affects an area of effect (AoE), the extra damage is halved.
> - **Failure:** The hellfire surges out of control and explodes in your hands. You suffer fire damage equal to **Hellfire Level d6**.
> 
> **Notes:**
> 
> - The additional damage is added to the spell’s total, stacking with its normal effects.
> - This ability cannot be used again until you complete a long rest.
