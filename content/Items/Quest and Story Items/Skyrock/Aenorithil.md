The *Aenorithil* is a fist-sized dodecahedron made of deep emerald, its rich green facets almost glowing in any light. Each of the twelve faces is immaculately smooth, catching light in mesmerizing ways that make it appear as if shadows of forest and mountains move within. The color deepens toward the center, where faint golden veins run, like threads of molten rock, glimmering softly in a pulse-like rhythm.

Ancient, delicate engravings cover each face, in symbols so eroded with time that they’re almost unrecognizable. Its aura is heavy, grounding, and profoundly steady, as if this gemstone holds a connection to something timeless, a force that goes beyond the material realm. In the quietest moments, those who hold *Aenorithil* might catch the faintest tremor within, almost as if it resonates with the deep roots of the earth.
